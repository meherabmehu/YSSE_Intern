# Demo walkthrough

Start the portal with `npm start` (`npm.cmd start` in restricted Windows PowerShell), then open http://127.0.0.1:3000.

Use sample information. This is not a live application to YSSE.

## Explore the public portal

1. Open the homepage and explore departments, learning and updates.
2. On Departments, search for `web`, then open IT to see proposed responsibilities and hierarchy.
3. On Learning, filter by category or department. Open a course, switch lessons and download its worksheet.
4. On Internship, review the sample batch, eligibility, deadline and recruiting departments.
5. On Contact, follow official social and certificate-verification links.

## Complete one recruitment journey

Use two browser profiles or one regular and one private window so account sessions stay separate. The password for both local demo accounts is `YsseDemo2026!`.

| Role      | Email                    |
| --------- | ------------------------ |
| Applicant | applicant@demo.ysse.test |
| Staff     | staff@demo.ysse.test     |

1. Sign in as applicant. Open Internship → Start your demo application.
2. Fill the fields and select one department. Attach a sample PDF CV (up to 2 MB) or provide an HTTPS portfolio link.
3. Save draft, reload, and confirm that your details remain saved.
4. Confirm eligibility, weekly commitment and sample-data consent. Review and submit. Your reference and Submitted status appear in the dashboard.
5. In the other browser profile, sign in as staff. Open the application and save a score and private review notes.
6. Move the application to Shortlisted, then schedule an interview with a future time, interviewer and HTTPS meeting link.
7. Mark the interview complete, then select the candidate. The staff view now waits for applicant acceptance.
8. In the applicant dashboard, press Refresh. Review the interview/history and notification previews. Private evaluation notes are not visible.
9. Accept the demo offer. In the staff view, refresh and complete onboarding.
10. Refresh the applicant dashboard to see the welcome message and department assignment.

Alternative outcomes include waitlisting, rejection and applicant withdrawal. An applicant waitlisted before an interview cannot skip straight to selection. Selected applicants must accept before staff can onboard them.

## Staff controls

- Filter applications by search, department and stage; export the current results as CSV.
- Open Batch settings to configure eligibility, duration, weekly commitment, deadline, recruiting departments and whether applications are open.
- Open Announcements to add or remove a sample update. It appears on Home and Updates.

Times display in Asia/Dhaka. Date/time input fields use the device timezone and convert to UTC for storage.

## Boundaries

The demo previews notifications and offers; it does not send emails, create meeting invitations, verify identity, issue official certificates or publish to Facebook. Learning content, hierarchy and visual branding are proposed samples. Approved course recordings and real team profiles need YSSE content.

The seeded applicant can submit once per batch. To explore another application, create another demo account with a different sample email. The demonstration configures one batch; multiple-batch creation and historical batch administration remain future extensions.
