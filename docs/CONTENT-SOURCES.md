Department descriptions, goals, reporting roles, course readings/exercises, department emblems, demo announcements and batch details are proposed demonstration content. No real member names, testimonials, impact metrics or active deadlines are invented.

The demo batch deadline defaults to 30 days after initial database creation and can be edited by staff. It is not an official YSSE application deadline. Published historical batches differ in duration and department openings; those values are configurable.

Required before production: current team directory, approved hierarchy, actual responsibilities, course catalogue and recordings, current recruitment circular and eligibility, policy documents, approved brand assets and confirmed integration access.
